# Wow, that's a lot of Experiences!
I definitely think that my biggest success this learning cycle was LTI. I had a total of 15 experiences (as of writing this) and included in that was 3 shadow days and 12 interviews (with one upcoming shadow day not included). Considering that this LC was only 12 weeks long, that means that I was out and about 62.5% of the LTI days. The previous LC, I was only out 27% of the LTI days. Most importantly, that is a 114% increase in LTI experiences between LC2 and LC3. 

# LTI Highlights

### Site Workshop
At the very beginning of the LC, I went on a shadow day with Allison Ong at site workshop. I shadowed her on a site visit to a project in the Intl District, and got to meet the others from the site visit (I still see some of them around when I am in Chinatown!!). 
### 9Zero
To the flipside of Site Workshop, most of my 9Zero experiences were pretty late along in the LC. I initially got their contact info from Andrea Oferrall, a substitute for an LTI day and a teacher at my elementary school. She talked about how they are focused on climate activism and neighboring topics, and a spark lit up. I went on an interview, loved it, went on a shadow day, loved it even more, and now, Chrissy and I are planning a visit to 9Zero for some BP students to attend. So exciting!
