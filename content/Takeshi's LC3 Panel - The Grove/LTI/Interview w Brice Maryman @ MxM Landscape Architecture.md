# Interview Reflection
##### Which questions were the best? Why?
I think that the follow up questions about some projects he is working on were the best ones. It really lit a spark in me and he got to talk about what he loves.

##### What are you most proud of from the experience? Why?

##### What can you improve on for your next interview experience? Who can help with this?
I can take the time I need to ask more questions instead of filling the empty space with the bad questions that I talked about earlier.
