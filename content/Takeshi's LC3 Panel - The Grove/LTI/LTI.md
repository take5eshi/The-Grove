# What is LTI?
### Intro
LTI stands for Learning Through Internships. Now if you haven't heard of this before, you are probably wondering, why in the world is this 9th grader learning through internships???, and in your defense, it is a very valid question, but the meaning goes beyond just a simple answer. 

### Meaning
LTI is defined as the following in the [9th grade panel expectations](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12p_BR8qZlVuspc6pcJ6FHQ8XWVklB2EgFxrA8mgRbV0/edit?usp=sharing) document:
- Meaningful learning from adult experts not part of your normal network, e.g. industry professionals. Not just family or school folks.
- Networking skills - setting up interviews and conversations, being places, effective communication throughout.
- Developing skills appropriate to the industry (or situation) you’re trying to learn about.

But more broadly, LTI is learning about topics by experiencing what they look like as a career. Whether it is an interview or an internship doesn't matter, you are taking this career that lives in your dreams in a fantastical world, and you are pulling it down to reality, and that is very valuable.

---
### Examples
This usually looks like one of the following:
##### Interview
A baseline connection with somebody. Students will reach out and ask for an interview before proceeding to any higher level of LTI. Typically 30 minutes to an hour, either in-person or via zoom. Students will ask questions and take notes or record the meeting. After, students will complete an interview reflection, reflecting on what they learned.
##### Shadow Day
If an interview goes particularly well, a student can pitch a shadow day to the contact. These are much longer, typically 3-6 hours, and involve the student shadowing the contact in their day-to-day life. 
##### Internship
Lastly, if a shadow day goes particularly well, a student can pitch an internship. This is a twice a week experience that involves the student taking on responsibilities at the site and learning through experiencing what it is like to work there.

---

##### An Important Detail
An important detail about LTI is that it is mostly run via a platform called [ImBlaze](https://www.imblaze.org/). [ImBlaze](https://www.imblaze.org/) is a web/mobile app that has a database of hundreds of contacts in our area, that have already said yes to an interview/shadow day/internship.  Then, all of our emails, experiences, and notes will be tracked in an [[LTI Tracker]].

### Timeline
We started out the year by going through the process of learning how to effectively do LTI work. This was called "LTI Certification." Basically, we learned how to survive in the real world, and once they were sure we could do it, they tossed us out there and we did our best to figure it out. 

After, we spend every Tuesday and Thursday reaching out, or having an experience. Internships will occur on Tuesdays and Thursdays. 

In the middle of the year, 9th graders started the LTI Zone for the first time in their high school career. The zone is a focused area specifically designed for getting students out and about, and hopefully for a long time. This last LC, we were partnered with the 301s for the zone. While most students typically hate the zone (me included), it is still a great tool for getting out, as shown through my vast increase in LTI experiences and more.
