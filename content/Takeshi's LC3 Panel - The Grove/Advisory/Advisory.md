# What is Advisory?
### Intro
At its core, Advisory work is work that I do during advisory time, but that is a very flimsy definition, that doesn't serve much purpose. Advisory work is goals that come off of my lp, or work assigned in class, that we have time allotted for. Usually the assigned work is guided by an advisor, and the goals are independent, worked on in my own time, or during dedicated IWT.
