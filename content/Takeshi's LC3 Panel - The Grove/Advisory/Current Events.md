A notable class activity from LC3 was our work on current events. After the completion of the Literary Analysis Essay and surrounding topics, we started the unit by voting on some current events that interested us, sorting into groups based on that (I was in the immigration group), reading [an article](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aH1zaEeriahxWSxs2GCkhWwGq-VssPSPfCvsnBpDwd4/edit?usp=sharing), and exploring it further through different levels of comprehension: 
 1. [Knowledge](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-YlGXpJmHKj0VZrMR6qf87Stv2PBLiUAdGJVNGD_lrk/edit?usp=sharing)
 2. [Understanding](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1m08ObiwjM1ZZoUTrhN7TSo5AnL3hG7ogg5EQ3yRKQPA/edit?usp=drive_link)
 3. [Application](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OD_qq_2VdguVYshoircez23jnPCWUt6l221GVpJ6lus/edit?usp=sharing)
 4. [Analysis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OD_qq_2VdguVYshoircez23jnPCWUt6l221GVpJ6lus/edit?usp=sharing)
 5. [Synthesis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YwsBUdZs7FLCRTuZhLo6Lzqe2Z6llK0Fm-Ozu0miLK4/edit?usp=sharing)
 6. [Evaluation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YwsBUdZs7FLCRTuZhLo6Lzqe2Z6llK0Fm-Ozu0miLK4/edit?usp=sharing)
